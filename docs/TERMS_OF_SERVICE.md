# MacroLens Terms of Service

**Prototype draft — last updated October 30, 2026**

These terms describe use of the MacroLens nutrition-tracking prototype. By continuing, you agree to use it for personal, informational tracking.

## Prototype access

MacroLens currently provides a local demonstration. Sign-in and account creation are prototype flows only. Credentials are not verified, transmitted, or stored, and no account is created.

## Nutrition information

Food recognition, portion estimates, calorie totals, and macronutrient values are approximate and may be incomplete or wrong. The image model suggests food labels; displayed nutrition is calculated from the sample food catalog after you confirm a match and portion.

## Not medical advice

MacroLens is for general informational tracking only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for medical or dietetic advice. Consult a qualified professional for personal health decisions.

## Acceptable use

Use only photos you have permission to use. Do not use the prototype for emergencies, clinical decisions, or to process another person's information without their permission.

## Availability and changes

This prototype is provided for testing and may be changed or unavailable at any time. Data can be lost when the app closes or is reinstalled.

## Contact and release readiness

This prototype has no connected support or account service. Before public release, the operator should add its legal name and contact address, set an effective date, and have these terms reviewed for the intended markets.
