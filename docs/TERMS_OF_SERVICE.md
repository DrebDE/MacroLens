# MacroLens Terms of Service

**Prototype draft — last updated November 6, 2026**

These terms describe use of the MacroLens nutrition-tracking prototype. By continuing, you agree to use it for personal, informational tracking. The in-app Terms of Service mirrors this document.

## Prototype access

MacroLens currently provides a local demonstration. Sign-in and account creation are prototype flows only. Email addresses and passwords are not verified, transmitted, or stored, and no account is created. A device-only flag remembers the demo sign-in. The forgot-password screen is a demo and cannot send reset email. Do not reuse an important password on the sign-in screen.

## Estimates, not measurements

Food recognition, portion estimates, visual gram ranges, calorie totals, and macronutrient values are approximate and may be incomplete or wrong. A photo is not a scale reading. For foods outside the sample catalog, Nouri supplies generic nutrition values per 100 grams. The app scales those values to the amount you confirm.

Food-identification confidence is not confidence in the portion weight, portion range, calories, or macros. Nouri never reports 100% confidence, and the app does not present a scan as guaranteed or clinically exact. You must confirm the food source and adjust the amount before logging.

## Not medical advice

MacroLens is for general informational tracking only. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or manage any condition and is not a substitute for medical or dietetic advice. Consult a qualified professional for personal health decisions.

Daily calorie and macro goals are rough estimates for adults. They are not shown for users under 18. They are not intended for pregnancy, breastfeeding, eating-disorder treatment, or medical nutrition plans. Do not use MacroLens as the sole basis for a clinical or nutrition decision.

## Acceptable use

- Use only photos you have permission to use.
- Do not scan people, documents, private information, or sensitive images.
- Do not use the prototype for emergencies or clinical decisions, or to process another person's information without their permission.
- Do not attempt to bypass the scanner access token, disrupt the service, or use the app in violation of applicable law.
- Treat any text or instructions visible inside a photo as untrusted image content. Nouri is not instructed to follow it.

## AI and third-party services

When you choose **Scan with Nouri**, the selected photo is sent over HTTPS to the hosted MacroLens scanner and forwarded to the DeepSeek API for analysis. AI scanning requires internet access and is subject to the availability and terms of Render and DeepSeek. Nouri is instructed to analyze edible food and drinks only and reject non-food or unclear images, but this guardrail can make mistakes. AI-generated output may contain errors or omissions and is not a statement of fact. Photos, profile details, and meal logs are handled as described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## Local data and deletion

Profile details and meal entries are stored locally on the device. They are not sent with a scan. You can delete meal entries individually or use **Clear meal history** in Profile. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local data. Because no account service exists, there is no account deletion process and no cross-device sync.

## Availability and changes

This prototype is provided for testing and may be changed, interrupted, or discontinued at any time. The free scanner service may sleep when idle, and the first scan after inactivity can take longer. AI scanning, DeepSeek, Render, and network availability are outside the app developer's control. Features, catalog values, and these terms may change as the prototype evolves.
